# Miku in 0.1

Guess the Vocaloid song from a 0.1-second clip.

- About 1000 songs. Easy is the mega-hits everyone knows; the other levels follow VocaDB popularity
- Type your guess: the box autocompletes English, romaji and Japanese titles, and producers
- Four tries per song: a wrong guess or Skip unlocks a longer clip (0.1s, 0.5s, 1s, 3s) for fewer points
- Playback starts at the first sound of the preview, or at a random spot
- Get one right and the song plays full screen until you hit New song

Clips stream from Apple Music's 30-second previews, so it needs an internet connection, and "from the start" means the start of Apple's preview, which for most songs is partway into the track. Song popularity data comes from [VocaDB](https://vocadb.net).
