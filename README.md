# Miku in 0.1

Guess the Vocaloid song from a 0.1-second clip.

- About 1000 songs. Easy is the mega-hits everyone knows; the other levels follow VocaDB popularity
- Songs play from their original Niconico upload, from the very start of the song
- Type your guess: the box autocompletes English, romaji and Japanese titles, and producers
- Four tries per song: a wrong guess or Skip unlocks a longer clip (0.1s, 0.5s, 1s, 3s) for fewer points
- Get one right and the music video plays full screen until you hit New song
- If a video opens with silence, "Heard nothing? Start a bit later" moves that song's start (remembered per song)

Needs an internet connection. Song data comes from [VocaDB](https://vocadb.net); audio and video play through Niconico's embedded player.
